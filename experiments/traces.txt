Each system lives in a world with two food patches. 'yields' = food available at patch 1 and patch 2 this step.
Each active unit costs 1 food per step; an active motor unit collects the yield of its patch. 'store' = food in reserve. If store would go below 0 the system dies.
Unit roles: S(pN)=sensor that fires when patch N is the richer one, R=sensor of its own low store, M(pN)=motor that harvests patch N, I=internal unit.
